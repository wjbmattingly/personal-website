Chai is a component-based processing engine for AI workflows, built for Yale University's cultural heritage collections across its libraries, archives, and museums. A workflow is a single JSON tree of components (OCR, detection, extraction, classification, translation, retrieval) that Chai runs while keeping every intermediate result traceable, reviewable, and correctable. It is the core of a family of open-source tools that includes a drag-and-drop workflow builder, a UI builder that exports standalone apps, and ready-made workflows for alt-text generation and audio transcription.
